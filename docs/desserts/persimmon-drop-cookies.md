---
title: Persimmon Drop Cookies
makes: about 4 dozen
time: 15 min bake
---

## Method

1. Stir the baking soda into the persimmon pulp and set aside.
    - 1 cup persimmon, peeled and cut up
    - 1 tsp baking soda
2. Sift the flour and add the other dry ingredients.
    - 2 cups flour
    - 1 tsp cinnamon
    - ½ tsp cloves
    - ½ tsp nutmeg
    - ½ tsp salt
    - 1 cup chopped walnuts
    - 1 cup raisins
3. Cream the shortening and sugar. Add the egg and vanilla and mix well.
    - ½ cup shortening (or 1 stick margarine)
    - 1 cup sugar
    - 1 egg, beaten
    - 1 tsp vanilla
4. Add the dry ingredients and the persimmon alternately, starting with the flour.
5. Drop onto a greased cookie sheet and bake at 160 °C / 325 °F for about 15 minutes. Grease the sheet only once.
