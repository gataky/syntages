---
title: Pumpkin Cake
time: 2 hr
---

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 160 °C / 325 °F and flour a cake pan.
2. Mix the dry ingredients well.
    - 1½ cups sugar
    - 1⅔ cups flour
    - ½ tsp baking soda
    - ½ tsp baking powder
    - 3 tsp pumpkin pie spice
3. Add the rest of the ingredients and mix.
    - 2 eggs, beaten
    - 1 cup pumpkin
    - ½ cup oil
    - ½ cup water
    - ½ cup chopped nuts
4. Pour into the pan and bake for 1½–2 hours.
