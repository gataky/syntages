---
title: California Carrot Cake
makes: 3 × 20 cm (8″) layers
time: 1 hr
---

## Method

### Cake

1. Heat the oven to 180 °C / 350 °F.
2. Drain the pineapple, keeping the juice for the icing, and mix it with the carrots and nuts. Set aside.
    - 1 can crushed pineapple (8 oz)
    - 2 cups grated carrots
    - ½ cup finely chopped nuts
3. Sift together the flour, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda and salt. Set aside.
    - 240 g flour
    - 5.2 g cinnamon
    - 8 g baking powder
    - 6 g baking soda
    - 7 g salt
4. Beat the sugar, eggs and oil until fluffy.
    - 240 g sugar
    - 4 eggs
    - 97.5 g oil
5. Add the sifted dry ingredients and blend well.
6. Stir in the carrot mixture until thoroughly combined.
7. Pour into 3 well-greased 20 cm (8″) round pans and bake for 35–40 minutes.

### Icing

1. Beat the margarine, cream cheese and vanilla until fluffy.
    - 113 g margarine
    - 227 g cream cheese (one 8 oz package)
    - 1 tsp vanilla
2. Slowly add the powdered sugar, beating all the time.
    - 1 box powdered sugar
3. If it's too stiff to spread, beat in 1 tbsp of the pineapple juice.
