title: Peanut Butter Pie
time: 1 hr
---

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C / 400 °F.
2. Blend the filling ingredients and pour into the pastry shell.
    - 1 cup light or dark corn syrup
    - 3 eggs, lightly beaten
    - ⅓ cup peanut butter
    - 1 cup sugar
    - ½ tsp vanilla
    - 1 unbaked [pie shell](../pie-crust/), 23 cm (9″)
3. Bake for 15 minutes, then reduce the heat to 180 °C / 350 °F and bake for 30–35 minutes more. The centre should look slightly less set than the edges.
