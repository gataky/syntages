---
title: Banana Cream Pie
time: 20 min + 2 hr chilling
---

## Method

1. Let the cream cheese soften to room temperature.
    - 227 g cream cheese (one 8 oz package)
2. Line the crust with slices from 2 of the bananas. Keep the third for the top.
    - 1 crumb crust or prebaked [pie shell](../pie-crust/), 23 cm (9″)
    - 3 medium bananas
3. Whip the cream cheese until fluffy. Gradually add the condensed milk and keep stirring until well blended.
    - 1 can sweetened condensed milk (Eagle Brand)
4. Add the lemon juice and vanilla and blend well.
    - ⅓ cup lemon juice
    - 1 tsp vanilla
5. Pour into the crust and chill for 2 hours, until firm.
6. Just before serving, slice the last banana over the top.
