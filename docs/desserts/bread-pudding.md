---
title: Bread Pudding
time: 1 hr 10 min
---

## Method

1. Put the bread in a greased baking dish.
    - 2 cups cubed stale bread
2. Mix the rest of the ingredients and pour them over the bread.
    - 3 eggs, lightly beaten
    - ½ cup sugar
    - 1½ tsp vanilla
    - ¼ tsp salt
    - 2 cups milk
3. Set the dish in a shallow pan of hot water and bake at 180 °C / 350 °F for about 1 hour, until a knife inserted in the centre comes out clean.
4. Serve with whipped cream or hard sauce.
