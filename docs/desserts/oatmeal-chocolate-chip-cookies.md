---
title: Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Cookies
time: 20 min bake
---

## Method

1. Cream the butter and oil.
    - 110 g butter
    - 75 g olive oil
2. Add the sugars one at a time, creaming after each.
    - 80 g granulated sugar
    - 60 g brown sugar
3. Add the eggs one at a time, beating after each.
    - 2 eggs
4. Add the hot water and vanilla and mix well.
    - 1 tbsp hot water
    - 1 tsp vanilla
5. Slowly mix in the flour, salt and baking soda.
    - 180 g flour
    - 5 g salt
    - 6 g baking soda
6. Add the oats, chocolate chips, coconut and nuts and mix thoroughly.
    - 100 g oats
    - 220 g chocolate chips
    - 50 g shredded coconut
    - 100 g chopped nuts
7. Drop 2 tbsp portions onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
8. Bake at 180 °C / 350 °F for about 20 minutes. Or rest the dough in the fridge for a day or two first, to let the flavours develop.
9. Cool on a rack.
