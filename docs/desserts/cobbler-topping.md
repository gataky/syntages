---
title: Cobbler Topping
makes: 8 pieces
time: 30 min
---

A quick puff-pastry topping for fruit cobbler.

## Method

1. Cut the margarine into the Bisquick.
    - 1 cup Bisquick
    - ¼ cup margarine
2. Add the boiling water and stir with a fork until the mixture comes together.
    - 3 tbsp boiling water
3. Divide into 8 pieces and pat them out between sheets of waxed paper.
4. Bake at 220 °C / 425 °F for 20–25 minutes.
