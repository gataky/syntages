---
title: Ethiopian Mushrooms
time: 30 min
---

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a medium skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until translucent.
    - 1 tbsp oil
    - 160 g red onion, thinly sliced
2. Add the mushrooms, rosemary and a good pinch of salt and cook until the mushrooms start to turn golden at the edges. Stir now and then, and add a splash of water if the pan gets too dry.
    - 385 g mushrooms, chopped
    - ½ tsp dried rosemary
3. Mix the spices, garlic and salt into the wine and water, then add it to the skillet.
    - 2 tsp berbere spice blend (see notes)
    - 1 tsp paprika
    - 2 cloves garlic, minced
    - 1 tsp ground coriander
    - ½ tsp ground cardamom
    - a good pinch each of cinnamon, clove and nutmeg
    - ¼ tsp onion powder
    - 2 tbsp red wine (or 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar)
    - 60 ml water
    - ½ tsp salt
4. Mix well, cover and cook until the mushrooms are done to your liking.
5. Taste and adjust. For a saucier dish, add more water and simmer for a few minutes. Serve with flatbread, toast or injera, with [Ethiopian red lentils](../ethiopian-red-lentils/) on the side.

## Notes

- To make it a meal, add 1 cup of cooked chickpeas halfway through simmering, mash them lightly and keep cooking.
- **Berbere blend:** mix 1½ tsp paprika (or New Mexico chili powder), ½ tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp cumin, ⅛ tsp fenugreek, ½ tsp cardamom, ½ tsp onion flakes, ¼ tsp black pepper and a pinch each of cinnamon and clove.
