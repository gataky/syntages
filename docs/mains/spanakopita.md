---
title: Spanakopita
serves: 10–12
time: 40 min prep · 45–60 min bake
---

Spinach pie with feta cheese.

## Method

### Dough

1. Beat everything in a stand mixer with the dough hook on medium-high for a few minutes, until the dough is soft, elastic and comes away from the sides of the bowl. Without a mixer, knead by hand for 5 minutes.
    - 500 g durum flour (or all-purpose flour)
    - 100 ml olive oil
    - 20 ml white wine vinegar
    - 10 g granulated sugar
    - 1½ tsp salt
    - 210 g water
2. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and rest at room temperature for 30 minutes.

### Filling

1. Wash the vegetables and herbs thoroughly.
2. Coarsely chop the spring onions and herbs.
    - 3 spring onions
    - ½ bunch mint
    - 1 bunch cilantro
    - ⅓ bunch dill
    - 4 tsp oregano
3. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the oil and spring onions and stir until softened.
    - 2 tbsp olive oil
4. Tear the spinach into pieces with your hands, add it to the pan and sauté until all the liquid has evaporated.
    - 1 kg spinach
5. Take the pan off the heat and tip the spinach into a large bowl. Add the herbs, lemon zest, feta (broken up by hand), cream cheese and plenty of salt and pepper. Mix and set aside.
    - 1 lemon, zested
    - 500 g feta cheese
    - 200 g cream cheese
    - salt and pepper

### Assembly

1. Heat the oven to 190 °C fan / 375 °F. Brush a 30 cm round baking pan with olive oil.
    - 200 ml olive oil, for brushing and drizzling
2. Divide the dough into 6 equal pieces. Sprinkle the counter with semolina and roll each piece into a sheet 40–50 cm across, adding more semolina if it sticks. The shape doesn't matter for the first 5 sheets, but the 6th closes the pie, so keep it neat.
    - fine semolina, for rolling
3. Cut 5 of the sheets crosswise into 4 pieces each.
4. Lay a piece on one side of the pan and drizzle it with oil without touching the dough with the brush. Add 3 more pieces, drizzling each, to cover the base.
5. Repeat with 2 more sheets (8 pieces) to build up the base. Make the walls first, then fill the gaps with dough to give it texture.
6. Spread the filling evenly over the base and drizzle with oil.
7. Lay the next 2 sheets (8 pieces) over the filling the same way, drizzling each piece.
8. Lay the last whole sheet on top, making folds and tucking the edges in with your hands.
9. Score the pie into pieces with a knife, drizzle with the rest of the oil and bake for 45–60 minutes, until crisp and golden.
10. Cut into pieces and serve.
