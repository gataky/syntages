---
title: Fried Chicken
time: 45 min
---

The amounts depend on how much chicken you're frying.

## Method

1. Wash the chicken thoroughly, pat it dry and sprinkle with salt.
    - cut-up chicken
    - salt
2. Mix the coating, using about equal amounts of flour and Bisquick.
    - flour
    - Bisquick
    - salt, pepper and paprika
    - a dash of oregano
3. Dip each piece in buttermilk and let the excess drip off, then coat it. Either shake the pieces with the coating in a large paper bag, or roll them in it in a bowl.
    - buttermilk
4. Fry in a heavy skillet in about 3 mm (⅛″) of hot shortening, turning the pieces as they brown. Don't leave them in one position too long or they may burn. But the chicken must be cooked all the way through, which takes about 30 minutes.
    - shortening, for frying
