time: 1 hr

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a medium pot, add the onion and cook over medium-high heat until golden brown, 8–10 minutes.
    - 3 tbsp olive oil
    - 1 large yellow onion, very finely diced
2. Add the garlic, tomato, tomato paste and berbere and cook for 5–7 minutes. Turn the heat down if it starts to catch.
    - 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
    - 1 Roma tomato, very finely chopped
    - 3 tbsp tomato paste
    - 2 tsp berbere
3. Add the lentils, broth and salt and bring to a boil. Cover, reduce the heat to low and simmer for about 40 minutes, stirring now and then and adding more broth if needed, until the lentils are soft.
    - 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
    - 2½ cups broth
    - 1 tsp salt
4. Drizzle with oil and simmer for a couple more minutes. Season with salt to taste.
    - 1 tbsp olive oil
