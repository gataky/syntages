---
title: Vegan Dahl
time: 45 min
---

## Method

### Dahl

1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Finely chop the shallots, garlic, ginger and chili and add them to the hot oil with the curry powder.
    - 0.5 dl rapeseed oil
    - 100 g banana shallots
    - 1 clove garlic
    - 25 g fresh ginger
    - ½ red chili
    - 1 tbsp Madras curry powder
2. Fry for 4–5 minutes without letting the onions colour much.
3. Rinse the carrot, cut it into small cubes and add it to the pot.
    - ½ carrot
4. Rinse the lentils, add them to the pot and fry briefly. Add the water, salt and lime leaves and cook until the lentils have absorbed the liquid.
    - 125 g red lentils
    - 3.5 dl water
    - ¾ tsp salt
    - 2 lime leaves
5. Add the tomatoes and coconut milk and keep cooking until it's as thick as you like. Season with salt and lemon juice.
    - 125 g canned tomatoes
    - ½ can coconut milk
    - 0.25 dl lemon juice

### Toppings

1. Quarter the cherry tomatoes.
    - 50 g cherry tomatoes
2. Toast the almonds in a dry pan for about 10 minutes, until crisp, then chop them.
    - 25 g roasted salted almonds
3. Top the dahl with the tomatoes, almonds and coriander.
    - ¼ bunch fresh coriander
