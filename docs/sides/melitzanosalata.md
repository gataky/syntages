---
title: Eggplant Dip (Melitzanosalata)
time: 1 hr 15 min
---

A simple recipe. Adjust everything to taste.

We've only ever used regular eggplants. Italian eggplants should work, but use two because they're smaller. We haven't tried Chinese eggplants. Their softer skin might make them easier, but it would take some experimenting.

## Method

1. Prick the eggplant with a fork about 10 times from top to bottom, so the steam can escape.
    - 1 large eggplant
2. Bake it on a rimmed tray at 200 °C / 400 °F for 1 hour, rotating the tray halfway. It's done when it's soft and has shrunk a little.
3. Peel back some of the skin and scoop the flesh into a big bowl with a spoon. Don't scrape too hard: a little skin is fine, but too much makes the texture unpleasant. Work while it's still warm if you can.
4. Add the pressed garlic, lemon juice, oil, salt and walnuts and mix vigorously.
    - 1 clove garlic
    - ½ lemon
    - 2 tbsp olive oil
    - ¼ tsp salt
    - chopped walnuts, to taste
5. Taste and adjust.
