---
title: Τσουρέκι
makes: 2 braided loaves
time: 5–6 hr, mostly rising
---

Tsoureki, Greek sweet Easter bread.

## Method

### Dough

1. Stir the yeast and a pinch of sugar into the lukewarm water.
    - 2 g dry yeast
    - 110 g lukewarm water
2. Grind the mastic and mahlab.
    - 3 g mastic
    - 10 g mahlab
3. Warm the butter, sugar and milk together until the butter melts and the sugar dissolves. It should be lukewarm.
    - 135 g butter, at room temperature
    - 200 g sugar
    - 150 g milk
4. Pour it into a large bowl and whisk in the eggs, then the yeast mixture.
    - 4 medium eggs, at room temperature (200 g without shells)
5. In the mixer bowl, combine the flour, ground mastic and mahlab, orange zest and the butter, egg and yeast mixture.
    - 870 g strong white flour
    - 1 orange, zested
6. Cover with plastic wrap and leave somewhere warm until at least doubled in size, about 3–4 hours.
7. Gently deflate the dough and cut it into 6 equal pieces, three for each loaf. Without flouring the counter, roll a piece a little with your hands, then hold it by the ends and shake it to stretch it into a rope. Repeat with the rest.
8. Braid each set of three ropes and put the loaves on a large baking tray lined with parchment paper. Let rise for 1 more hour.

### Garnish

1. Whisk the egg with the water and brush it over the loaves. Scatter with almond slivers.
    - 1 egg
    - 1 tbsp water
    - almond slivers
2. Bake in a preheated oven at 155 °C fan / 310 °F for 30–40 minutes, until browned and fluffy. If they brown too quickly, cover them with parchment paper and keep baking.
