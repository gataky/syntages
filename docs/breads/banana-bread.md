## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180 °C / 350 °F. Grease a loaf pan.
2. Blend everything except the chocolate chips in a high-speed blender or food processor until smooth.
    - 170 g oat flour
    - 4 large bananas
    - 3 large eggs
    - 4 tbsp maple syrup (or honey or agave nectar)
    - 1 tsp baking soda
    - 1 tbsp vanilla
3. Fold in the chocolate chips, if using.
    - 1/2 cup chocolate chips, optional
4. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 35–40 minutes, until a skewer comes out mostly clean.
5. Let it cool completely before slicing.
