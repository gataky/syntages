---
title: Dutch Baby
serves: 2–3
time: 30 min
---

One 25 cm (10″) puffed pancake, for breakfast.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C / 400 °F with a 25 cm (10″) cast-iron skillet inside, for at least 8 minutes.
2. Melt the butter. One tablespoon goes in the batter and two in the pan.
    - 3 tbsp butter
3. Blend the eggs, egg white, milk, 1 tbsp of the melted butter, sugar, flour, vanilla, cinnamon and salt until smooth and creamy, about 30 seconds. Scrape down the sides if needed.
    - 2 eggs, at room temperature
    - 1 egg white, at room temperature
    - ⅔ cup milk, at room temperature
    - 24 g granulated sugar
    - 80 g all-purpose flour
    - ¼ tsp vanilla extract
    - ¼ tsp cinnamon
    - ¼ tsp kosher salt
4. Take the skillet out of the oven (the handle is hot). Pour in the remaining 2 tbsp of melted butter and swirl to coat the bottom, then gently pour in the batter without splashing the sides.
5. Return it to the oven and bake for 20 minutes. Don't open the door to peek: even a crack can make it fall. It puffs up around the edges, sometimes enough to hide the centre.
6. Ease it onto a large plate with a thin spatula. It may sink a little, which is normal. Cut into wedges and serve with powdered sugar, more cinnamon and a squeeze of lemon.
    - powdered sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice, to serve
