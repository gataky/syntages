---
title: Carrot Soup
time: 1 hr
---

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C / 400 °F. Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Peel the carrots and cut them on the diagonal into pieces about 1 cm (½″) thick at the widest part.
    - 900 g carrots
3. Toss the carrots on the baking sheet with the oil and salt, then spread them in a single layer.
    - 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
    - ½ tsp fine sea salt
4. Roast until caramelised at the edges and easily pierced with a fork, 25–40 minutes, tossing halfway. Heirloom carrots can be done in 25 minutes; regular carrots are denser and usually need 35–40.
5. When the carrots are nearly done, warm the oil in a Dutch oven or soup pot over medium heat until shimmering. Add the onion and salt and cook, stirring now and then, until soft and translucent, 5–7 minutes.
    - 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
    - ¼ tsp fine sea salt
    - 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
6. Add the garlic and spices and stir until fragrant, 30 seconds to 1 minute. Pour in the broth, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom.
    - 2 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
    - ½ tsp ground coriander
    - ¼ tsp ground cumin
    - 4 cups vegetable broth (or water)
7. Add the roasted carrots, bring to a boil, then simmer gently for 15 minutes.
8. Take the pot off the heat and let it cool for a few minutes. Transfer to a blender, in batches if needed. Don't fill it past the max line or it can overflow.
9. Add the butter, lemon juice and pepper and blend until completely smooth. Taste, and add salt, pepper, more butter for richness or more lemon for zing. Blend again and serve.
    - 1–2 tbsp unsalted butter
    - 1½ tsp lemon juice
    - black pepper

## Notes

Keeps for about 4 days in the fridge, covered, or several months in the freezer.

## Variations

**Carrot ginger.** Add freshly grated ginger with the garlic.

**Curried.** Add Indian curry powder with the other spices, and ginger too if you like.

**Thai curried.** Add Thai red curry paste, and blend in lime juice instead of lemon at the end. Garnish with chopped peanuts and cilantro.
